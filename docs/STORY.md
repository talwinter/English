# Lost in the Game: series bible

## The idea in one line

In Grandpa's attic there is an old 1980s computer with a game called **THE LAST QUEST**. When the hero types
his name, the screen pulls him inside. Everyone in the game speaks only English. To get home he must find the
**7 Save Disks** before **the Glitch** deletes every world, and him with it.

Learning English isn't homework here. It's how the hero gets home.

## Characters

- **The hero.** A modern teenager in a red hoodie, jeans and sneakers, dropped into fantasy worlds, which is
  funny every time. The player names him; let your son choose the name.
- **BYTE.** A tiny flying mouse cursor with eyes, and the hero's guide. BYTE speaks simple English and makes jokes.
  BYTE can translate single words ("Click a word and I translate it. It costs me energy, so not every word,
  OK?"). This is the in-story reason for click-to-translate and hints.
- **The Glitch.** The villain: a flickering shadow made of static and broken pixels. It's eating the game worlds
  and wants to escape into the real internet. It appears at the end of episodes as the cliffhanger.
- **Grandpa.** He wrote THE LAST QUEST in 1987, and he disappeared years ago. Notes he left inside the game
  (written in English, of course) are reading puzzles, and the season-long mystery is where he is.

## The worlds (one per season)

Each world is a different classic game style, so the series never gets boring.

| # | World | Inspired by | English focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The North**: Viking village, wolves, an ice giant | God of War, King's Quest | things around us, commands (`take`, `open`), `there is` |
| 2 | **The Castle**: knights, a dragon, a princess who is the real hero | King's Quest | colors and adjectives, `can / can't`, where things are (`in, on, under`) |
| 3 | **Pirate Island**: sword fights won with comebacks | Monkey Island | past tense (`I found`, `you were`), questions |
| 4 | **Space Station**: robots and alarms | Space Quest | numbers, time, instructions, `will` |
| 5 | **The Haunted House** | Maniac Mansion | feelings, body, describing people |
| 6 | **The Desert Temple**: traps and maps | Indiana Jones | directions, comparing (`bigger`, `the best`) |
| 7 | **The Core**, inside the computer | all of them | everything, for the final battle |

## Season 1: The North

Every episode ends on a cliffhanger, then the daily Word Challenge.

0. **Insert Disk** (prologue, real world). The attic, the old computer, a dusty floppy disk. He types his name
   and gets pulled in. BYTE appears. *Words: computer, box, old, light, game, name, type, press.*
1. **The Cold Village** (built). Byte brings him to a village in the game: the game gave the hero a house, a
   grandmother and a father here (they are game characters, and they know him as their own). A key under a rock,
   Grandmother, the sword and the bread, the hungry wolf.
   *Cliffhanger: a voice in the forest: "It is your father!"*
2. **Into the Forest.** His game-father is trapped under a fallen tree. He needs rope and help from a bear.
   *Cliffhanger: the Glitch deletes the path behind them.*
3. **The Frozen Lake.** He crosses thin ice (a fast "read and choose" moment), trades with a fisherman, and finds
   Grandpa's first note.
4. **The Troll Bridge.** The troll asks riddles in English. Wrong answer: into the water!
5. **The Ice Cave.** It's dark: make a torch. Old runes turn out to be English signs.
6. **The Giant.** Boss battle: a word duel against the ice giant, with a timer. The fast part he'll love.
7. **Save Disk 1.** He wins the disk, the Glitch attacks, and a portal opens to the Castle world. *Season finale.*

## Continuity (every episode must follow from the one before)

- Start exactly where the last cliffhanger stopped. Byte is with the hero: he explains new things, never a stranger.
- Give every episode a `previously` of 2 to 4 very simple lines that say what happened before (the game says them
  at the start, with the voice).
- Never contradict an earlier episode: the real world is only the attic; everything else is inside the game.
- The game characters (Grandmother, the father) know the hero as their own; the hero knows he is in a game.

## How every episode is built (keep it fast)

- 3–4 scenes, 4–6 puzzles, 15–25 new words, **10–15 minutes**.
- Something happens on every click. A message is at most two short sentences.
- One action moment per episode: a chase, a word duel, or a timed choice.
- At least half the words come back from earlier episodes, so the story does the repeating.
- He can't skip: the next step always depends on understanding what he read.

## The daily rhythm (with the Discord lock)

- At 08:00 Discord locks.
- **Today's mission** is the next new episode if there is one, then the Word Challenge (8 quick questions,
  6 right to pass). With no new episode, it's just the Word Challenge on the words he has learned.
- When he passes, Discord opens until tomorrow morning.
- Aim for about **two new episodes a week**. On the other days the challenge keeps the words fresh.

## Ask your son

- What's the hero's name?
- What does the Glitch look like?
- Which world should come after The North?

If he helps make it, he'll want to play it.
