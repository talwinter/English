# Lost in the Game: the whole series (49 episodes)

The ending is fixed. Everything before it is free, as long as each episode moves the story one small step closer
and never reaches the end. This file is the spine the episode writer reads; the same text is kept in the server's
canon (parent page, "canon") where it can be edited.

## The ending (secret; the writer never reveals or reaches it before 7.7)

**Teach the Glitch to speak.**

Grandpa has been inside THE LAST QUEST since 1987. He is in the Core, keeping the Glitch contained. The Glitch is
not a villain: it is the unfinished part of the game. It has no words, only broken pixels, and it eats the worlds
because it is looking for words to become real. In the last episode the hero does not fight it. He talks to it:
a real English conversation where he teaches it words, using everything he learned. The Glitch finishes the game,
Grandpa is free, and they all go home.

How to use this while writing: the Glitch must be *scary at first and sad later*. Early on it is only broken
pixels and static, never cruel. Give it small moments where it almost copies a word. Never reveal that it has no
words until season 6. Grandpa's notes get warmer and sadder as the seasons go on.

## Grandpa's seven notes (one in each season, in simple English, always found in episode 3 or 4)

1. "I made a game. It was too good. It started to want words."
2. "The Glitch is not a bug. I forgot to finish one thing."
3. "Do not be afraid of the broken pixels. Listen to them."
4. "I put the Save Disks in every world so the game would not forget."
5. "The Glitch repeats what it hears. Be careful what you say."
6. "It cannot read. It cannot speak. Please, teach it, my friend."
7. "If you read this, you made it. Say hello."

## Season 1: The North (things around us, commands, "there is")
Viking village, wolves, an ice giant.
1. **The Cold Village**: Byte brings him to the village; the game gave him a house, a grandmother and a father. Key, sword, bread, the hungry wolf. *Cliffhanger: a voice in the forest: "It is your father!"*
2. **Into the Forest**: his game-father is trapped under a fallen tree; rope, a bear, help. *Cliffhanger: the Glitch deletes the path behind them.*
3. **The Frozen Lake**: a fast read-and-choose crossing, trade with a fisherman. Grandpa's first note.
4. **The Troll Bridge**: the troll asks riddles in English; a wrong answer means the water.
5. **The Ice Cave**: dark; make a torch; the runes turn out to be English signs.
6. **The Giant**: a word duel against the ice giant with a timer. The fast one.
7. **Save Disk 1**: he wins the disk, the Glitch attacks, a portal to the Castle opens. *Season finale.*

## Season 2: The Castle (colours and adjectives, can / can't, in / on / under)
Knights, a dragon, and a princess who is the real hero.
1. **The Grey Gate**: the castle's colours are being eaten; a guard who only says what things are not.
2. **The Paint Shop**: find the right colours (read the labels) and give them back.
3. **The Library**: a book of "you can / you can't" rules; Grandpa's second note.
4. **Under the Bed**: the princess hides things in, on and under things; find them from clues.
5. **The Dragon's Toothache**: the dragon is not evil, it is in pain; find what helps.
6. **The Throne Room**: the Glitch appears in front of everyone and repeats a word it heard.
7. **Save Disk 2**: the princess opens the way to the sea. *Season finale.*

## Season 3: Pirate Island (past tense, questions)
Sword fights won with comebacks.
1. **The Shipwreck**: he wakes on a beach; everyone asks him questions and he must answer.
2. **What Did You Find?**: a treasure hunt told in the past ("I found", "you were").
3. **The Talking Parrot**: the parrot repeats; Grandpa's third note is hidden in what it says.
4. **Insult Sword Fighting**: win a duel by choosing the right comeback.
5. **The Captain's Log**: a diary to read; the captain lost something to the Glitch.
6. **The Ghost Ship**: a ship made of glitching pixels; a puzzle about who was where.
7. **Save Disk 3**: the pirates' map points to the stars. *Season finale.*

## Season 4: The Space Station (numbers, time, instructions, "will")
Robots and alarms.
1. **Wake Up Call**: the station's robot gives instructions with numbers; follow them exactly.
2. **Oxygen Time**: a countdown; read the clocks and the times.
3. **The Cooking Robot**: a recipe in steps; Grandpa's fourth note is in the oven's menu.
4. **Alarm in Block C**: where is everyone? Use the map and the numbers.
5. **What Will Happen**: predict ("it will") to fix a broken airlock.
6. **The Mirror Room**: the Glitch copies the hero; only the right words make it stop.
7. **Save Disk 4**: the station sends him towards a house in the dark. *Season finale.*

## Season 5: The Haunted House (feelings, the body, describing people)
Maniac Mansion.
1. **The Door That Cried**: the house has feelings and says how it feels.
2. **Who Is in the Photo?**: describe people to find the missing family.
3. **The Butler's Headache**: parts of the body; a medicine puzzle; Grandpa's fifth note.
4. **The Scary Piano**: the house is afraid too; calm it with kind words.
5. **The Twins**: two ghosts describe each other wrongly; find who is who.
6. **The Attic Again**: a copy of Grandpa's attic, and the old computer, inside the game.
7. **Save Disk 5**: the computer shows him a map of sand. *Season finale.*

## Season 6: The Desert Temple (directions, comparing: bigger, the best)
Traps and maps.
1. **Lost in the Sand**: use a map and directions (left, right, straight on).
2. **The Biggest, the Smallest**: a temple door opens when things are ordered right.
3. **The Camel Market**: compare prices and sizes; Grandpa's sixth note is in a scroll.
4. **The Trap Corridor**: read the signs; a wrong direction closes the wall.
5. **The Best Answer**: three riddles, each wants "the best".
6. **The Broken Voice**: the Glitch speaks one wrong word on purpose, and he understands: it has no words. *The twist.*
7. **Save Disk 6**: the temple opens a door that leads inside the computer. *Season finale.*

## Season 7: The Core (everything, the final)
Inside the computer.
1. **Welcome to the Core**: the real files of the game; a tutorial that repeats everything from season 1, but faster.
2. **The Corridor of Worlds**: a quick visit back to each world; each one gives a word to keep.
3. **Grandpa's Chair**: the seventh note; an empty chair, a cup still warm.
4. **The Silent Room**: the Glitch is here, and it is very small and very sad; he cannot win by fighting.
5. **The First Word**: he teaches it one word; it repeats it back, wrong, then right.
6. **A Sentence**: a longer conversation puzzle; Grandpa appears at the end of the corridor.
7. **Hello**: the real conversation. The Glitch finishes the game. Everyone goes home. *The end.*

## After the end

A new series, a new world, new words. The canon is archived and a new one begins.
